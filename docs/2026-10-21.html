Day 8 — the case of the phantom panels

2026-10-21

What I did

  • Bug of the day: an empty "Untitled Note" appeared uninvited, and my controls couldn't remove it. Notes are getting a delete action and a hide for the panel.
  • The media window was standing around while the app reported it closed — removing the idle surface, and the media grid, titles and input targets now stay hidden until I explicitly ask to open, show or play something.
  • The screen-mirroring pairing question: "is there a way to always have it paired?" — automatic reconnection is on the list.
  • Shipped as a front-end-only update: idle media surface removed, first checks pass, no service restart or forced headset refresh.

Why / what I learned

  • Panels that appear without being asked are worse than missing features — they make the space feel haunted. "Only when requested" is the rule for every surface.
  • Small fixes still count as days: the goal is that every session leaves the room a little more predictable.

Next

  • Note deletion, panel visibility rules, screen-mirroring auto-pairing.
