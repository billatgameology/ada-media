Why / what I learned

  • I'm officially starting the fourth attempt at Ada today. This is its first entry.
  • The frame for this attempt: Ada lives in the room. It's in the headset with me, in my actual scanned space — the grid, the outlines, every surface it can find and name. Voice in, voice out, and it runs its own little sub-agents while we work.
  • A workspace that survives a reload is the difference between a demo and a place to work. The first session was mostly plumbing — import, anchors, save, restore — and that plumbing is what lets every later session start in the same room instead of from scratch.
  • Attempt four gets a trail. If this one works, the day-by-day of how it got there — including everything that didn't work — is the interesting part.
  • Open development from the start: these notes go up as they're written, not once they're pretty.
