Why / what I learned

  • "Can you see this?" becomes a real question once screenshots work. The status lights mean I don't have to guess whether the headset is connected before asking for something.
  • A status light has to measure the thing it names. "Vision on" was permission, not a capture connection — showing them as separate lines fixed the lie.
  • Most of the day was the unglamorous middle: capture mismatches, snapback paths, stale status, a swallowed failure message. Failure paths deserve the same care as features, especially when the assistant is supposed to be trustworthy.
