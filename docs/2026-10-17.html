Why / what I learned

  • "A pause shouldn't mean you've finished thinking." That's the right bar for the interaction: hold my intent, don't make me hold its rules.
  • Ada's movement is basic right now, but it's headed somewhere deliberate: leave her on the desk, have her follow me around, or tell her a place and she hovers there. Position and animation are the visual cue that makes her feel present — the foundation for the embodiment work ahead.
    Evidence: com.oculus.browser-20261017-125302-0.mp4 @ 5:25–7:01.
  • The loop is becoming the product: issue in the headset → sub-agent builds the fix → release deploys → headset confirms it, all in one day. By closeout that was 1,349 tests and a live review-and-close.
  • The log itself is part of the build now. One note becomes the site and six platform drafts, but I still post by hand — the automation stops at the clipboard.
