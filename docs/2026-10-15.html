Why / what I learned

  • Hands are how a room becomes an interface. If I can point at a surface and have Ada know exactly what I mean, the whole space becomes addressable.
  • Watching the raw tracking data while building against it is the whole trick: you can't debug an interaction you can't see.
  • The headset-off stall is why work has to survive the headset leaving the head. Queued voice and single-owner audio came out of that.
  • Presets belong in the app, not in project memory — a dev view I can't recall isn't really a preset.
