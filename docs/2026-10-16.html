Why / what I learned

  • The room stops being a picture the moment you can edit it. Renaming a table is small; realizing you can delete it and rebuild the space is the door opening.
  • The bug of the day came from being too literal — deletion demanded an exact phrase. Intent, not incantations.
  • Delegation is not completion. The worker cubes made that visible: a task can be done without being reviewed, closed, or actually finished from my side.
  • Speech should be the chat, not a performance around it. One text, two surfaces, in order.
