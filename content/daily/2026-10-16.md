## What I did

- Ran the whole day as one loop: test in the headset, log the issue, spawn sub-agents, test again. Seven sessions between 15:51 and 20:33.
- Naming got solid — select a surface, tag it, rename it. Main desk, main screen, whiteboard, walls, server rack.
- And then the big one: delete, for real. A request in the evening session, live before that session ended. Eight surfaces deleted and five renames kept by the end of the day.
- The breakthrough moment: scene grid, blue outlines, name labels and the restored room all on at once. The room finally reads like an interface, not just a scan.
- Learned the model that makes it safe: the app edits its own saved copy of the room scan — a snapshot. Move furniture for real and it won't follow, and the original Quest scan is never touched.
- Fixed the speech mid-day: voice was cutting off, chat had junk "unspoken words," and replies landed as one giant text block. Now updates stream as they happen.
- Ended by saving workspace revision 5: 50 surfaces, every renamed label retained, every deleted surface confirmed gone. The test suite went 801 → 915 along the way.

## Why / what I learned

- The room stops being a picture the moment you can edit it. Renaming a table is a small thing; realizing you can delete it and rebuild the space is the door opening.
- The bug of the day came from the assistant being too literal — deletion demanded an exact phrase. "You shouldn't need a magic phrase." Intent, not incantations.

## Next

- File the queue of big fixes: the message queue needs to be visible and editable; sub-agents need to close themselves when they finish; and no more nine-minute stalls with zero feedback.
