## In the headset

- **The media library, cleaned up** — the photo and video previews lose their backing frames and persistent labels. File names appear only while hovered or grabbed, the huge “AdaXR Media / Close Media” header goes away, and video gets manual play/pause plus the correct aspect ratio immediately. The first rendered pass cleared 41 checks.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.browser-20261021-152543-0.mp4` @ 6:50–9:44, 10:55–11:42 and 14:12–15:05; `com.oculus.browser-20261021-200705-0.mp4` @ 0:30–3:46.*
- **The photo moves** — “move this photo to second desk” worked, after “second desktop” was rejected as an unmapped destination. The original instruction was clear; the translation into an executable action was the fragile part. A failed row now shows “failed, message kept,” but remove/resend are still missing.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.browser-20261021-200705-0.mp4` @ 3:57–8:57.*
- **The queue was never empty** — a combined request failed gateway validation and the queue hid failed entries, so it looked cleared. The requests were still retained, including media, screen share, share status and dialogue-history movement. The failure display and replay path are being repaired.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.vrshell-20261021-162516-0.mp4` @ 5:10–7:56.*
- **Close the loop** — seven finished workers were still counted as open because review closeout was not enforced. Separately, the screen mirror’s voice relocation sent it to the ceiling, and the dialogue-history panel reported busy while nothing was touching it.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.vrshell-20261021-162516-0.mp4` @ 0:00–1:46; `com.oculus.browser-20261021-172306-0.mp4` @ 0:00–5:38.*

## Why / what I learned

- “Empty” is a lie if failures are hidden. A queue that drops its failed rows looks calm and loses the only information I need.
- A move can succeed and still be wrong. The photo reached the second desk; the mirror reached the ceiling. Both are real evidence, and neither should be reported as a clean pass.
- Common actions need one clean path. Saved working calls beat repeated documentation searches and helper agents when the goal is simply “open the media library.”

## Next

- Add remove and resend to failed messages, and make the failure row impossible to miss.
- Make worker closeout part of the review, not a separate cleanup I have to remember.
- Finish voice relocation for menus, screens and dialogue history without the ceiling.
