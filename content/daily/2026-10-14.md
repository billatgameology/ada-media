## What I did

- Started the fourth attempt at Ada today — officially. This is its first entry.
- The frame for this attempt: Ada lives in the room. It's in the headset with me, in my actual scanned space — the grid, the outlines, every surface it can find and name. Voice in, voice out, and it runs its own little sub-agents while we work.
- Set the rules for this log: one entry per build day, written that day, honest about what broke, with the real quotes from real test sessions instead of a cleaned-up story later.

## Why / what I learned

- Attempt four gets a trail. If this one works, the day-by-day of how it got there — including everything that didn't work — is the interesting part. It's the part nobody usually gets to see.
- Open development from day one: these notes go up as they're written, not once they're pretty. The mistakes are part of the material.
