## What I did

- New rule in the design contract: every item should be relocatable by voice — not just Ada. The mirrored screen couldn't move to the second desk by name; that's now logged as an implementation gap, not a limitation.
- Wireless ADB now reconnects itself on a new XR session: one bounded startup attempt with the saved headset address, identity verified before showing green, no cable and no screenshot. Confirmed live — "the ADB wireless started up connected right away."
- Fixed the listening hang: after a quiet noise-only attempt, Ada returns to listening promptly instead of blocking my next sentence until a minute-long timeout.
- Fixed the silence on failure: when a spatial action failed, the action-verification gate swallowed the entire final reply. Now the explanation still arrives — deployed and tested.
- First real remote-control demo: from inside VR, Ada switched Chrome to the YC application tab, maximized it, typed our company name and a one-line description, saved, and verified after reload. "Yay! Cool, thanks. It worked."
- Built a spatial working-note flow for the application: Ada asks follow-up questions, captures decisions, keeps brainstorming separate from approved wording, saves on request — and fills the form only after approval. Never submit.
- Ada's first-pass answer, drafted from the build itself: "Gamology Space is building a context-aware interface for working with AI… AdaXR brings an AI collaborator into a mixed reality workspace."
- Screenshot review caught the working note rendering as a one-line preview, full text intact but presentation broken. Being fixed as wrapped, scrollable text — and finished task cubes that never close are being chased too.

## Why / what I learned

- The assistant stops being a chat window when it can operate the same tools I use. Chrome on the Mac is the first demo — point at the work, talk about it, let Ada do the typing.
- The YC form is a good forcing function: a real task with real stakes exposes everything the demo usually hides — notes that don't render, replies that don't arrive, cubes that won't close.
