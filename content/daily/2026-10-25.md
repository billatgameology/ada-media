## In the headset

- **The final run** — Ada loads the 30mm marker holder, places it, and sends a print sub-agent. Then she opens the YC application on the mirrored Mac, writes and saves the “what are we making” description, captures the tech-stack whiteboard, fills that section, verifies the draft survived a reload, and stops short of submitting.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.browser-20261025-101321-0.mp4` @ 0:00–9:37; `demo video yc.mp4` @ 0:00–9:20; `yc product demo 3 min.mp4` @ 0:00–2:57.*
- **The holder becomes a print** — the 20mm holder is one-to-one in size but too shallow for the eraser. The 30mm version gains a centered divider, exports to STL, and the sub-agent queues it in white PLA with an estimated print time of 2 hours 58 minutes. The stale print/worker cube is cleared and the STL is saved locally.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.browser-20261025-082848-0.mp4` @ 0:00–11:32; `com.oculus.browser-20261025-101321-0.mp4` @ 8:22–8:58.*
- **What the camera exposed** — contact grab is jittery while distance grab stays smooth; the loading delay was mostly pre-command thinking, not the model; the missing center divider was a real omission. The ambiguity guard rejects the existing 30mm design, and the conflict guard blocks a valid 10mm depth increase. A stale worker card forces a restart during the demo.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.browser-20261025-073229-0.mp4` @ 3:33–5:07 and 7:30–14:16; `com.oculus.browser-20261025-082848-0.mp4` @ 2:14–5:47; `com.oculus.browser-20261025-093843-0.mp4` @ 1:36–4:40.*
- **The draft survives** — the YC description is saved and still present after reload; the tech-stack answer is filled from the captured board. The application remains unsubmitted. The demo ends with the print job sent.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.browser-20261025-101321-0.mp4` @ 4:26–5:22 and 8:50–9:20.*

## Why / what I learned

- A live demo is an integration test with stakes. It finds the stale card, the ambiguous guard and the jitter that a checklist can miss.
- The print handoff is a useful end-to-end test because it crosses every boundary: a 3D model, a file export, a sub-agent, a slicer and a physical printer.
- The most damaging failure is not a missing feature. It is a valid request rejected by a guard that cannot tell the difference between “ambiguous” and “something moved.”
