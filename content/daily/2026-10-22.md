## In the headset

- **The first group move** — “move them to the whiteboard” moved all five media items. The position worked; the orientation did not. They kept their old viewing angles and stuck out of the board instead of lying flat against it. The fix is a board-relative placement: use the rectangle’s own left/right and up/down axes, rotate each face parallel to that plane, and keep it slightly in front to avoid clipping.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.browser-20261022-202414-0.mp4` @ 1:18–4:07.*
- **Multi-select belongs in the shared model** — the limitation was not that group move needs a special feature. The voice path required one selected or named file. The better model: understand which objects are meant, then apply their existing actions to the whole compatible set. A selection is not a permanent group.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.browser-20261022-175152-0.mp4` @ 2:26–6:11.*
- **The summon gesture** — hover to arm, then hold an open palm and curl/reopen the four non-thumb fingers twice in a come-here motion. The target arrives near the hand but is not auto-grabbed; it hovers until I take it. Selection and activation are separate, which should keep ordinary pointing from triggering a summon.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.browser-20261022-175152-0.mp4` @ 6:30–7:37.*
- **What already passes** — video playback starts while held and pauses on release, two-hand resizing keeps its proportions, and the per-item play/pause and close buttons work. Multi-select is still design-only, and the summon gesture itself did not respond in testing.
  *Evidence: `com.oculus.browser-20261022-203341-0.mp4` @ 1:19–2:43; `com.oculus.browser-20261022-202414-0.mp4` @ 4:47–7:09.*

## Why / what I learned

- Moving a set is not the same as arranging a set. The whiteboard has a plane; the objects need to know about it.
- “Select one or more, then say what to do” is a much better foundation than adding a bespoke command for every combination of objects.
- A summon should feel like an invitation, not a grab. Arriving near the hand, unheld, keeps the last decision with me.

## Next

- Apply board-relative orientation to every item in a group move.
- Implement multi-select as shared target composition.
- Build the palm-up summon with a deliberate two-curl confirmation and a visible ready cue.
