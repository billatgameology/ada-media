## What I did

- **Five full takes, two false starts** — the founder story got shorter and more specific with each pass. The final take names the four iterations: a round-trip voice with Gemini 1.5, camera access, the first OpenClaw attempt, and the current build where the pieces finally fit.

The repair keeps the relevant decision history across retained intentions and adds native custody for room geometry, anchor generation, tracking loss and owner identity. ## Why / what I learned

- “Verify” is a promise that the room in front of me is the room being saved. If an unrelated Help or Back can erase the interruption, the promise is empty.
- Adoption is not a refactor. It is the work of proving that each existing feature can publish what actually happened, in its own vocabulary, without inventing a second planner.
- The founder video and the engineering work want the same thing: a clear thread from what happened to what comes next.
